Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance in the W. P. Carey School of Business at Arizona State University.

My research interests are in household finance, corporate finance, and labor and finance.

I recently completed a post-doctoral fellowship in the Economics of an Aging Workforce at the National Bureau of Economic Research. I received my PhD in Financial Economics at MIT Sloan. Prior to that, I worked as a research assistant at the Boston Fed and as a consultant at PwC.
